# Surface brief — Home and discovery

Mode: Operate. Scope: GameVault home, catalog discovery, game records, and personal library.

Audience and task: gamers browse provider-backed modern and classic games, open a record, and track progress without losing their own choices. Success is a truthful, usable path from catalog to personal log. Content is external RAWG data only when configured; personal state belongs to its authenticated owner. PS2 and PS3 must remain explicit destinations. No deployment target or assets were supplied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Treat the home as a collector's catalog you can use, not a promotional streaming billboard; refuse to bury older systems in a generic filter.

OWN-WORLD: Inferred from the pinned premium dark brief and the assigned catalog-grid form: graphite field, warm paper-like type, restrained signal orange, numbered catalog indexing, and real game artwork. The grid is a navigation instrument, not a texture.

STORY: Visitors can scan a featured game, move into live catalog results, jump directly to PS2 or PS3, then open an honest detail record. Signed-in users see their own Continue Playing, backlog, and favorites.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact fixed navigation rail frames a cinematic game-art feature above a tightly indexed set of game covers. The game title and provider-backed facts lead; search and PS2/PS3 shortcuts are immediately reachable, not hidden below a hero carousel.

FORM: Candidate 6, catalog-cell system; seed key 8a07db9f. The interface receives the system's whole-cell responsive catalog grid and direct browse indices while translating its newsprint ground to the documented dark premium environment. Code-led; no image-generation capability was reported.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

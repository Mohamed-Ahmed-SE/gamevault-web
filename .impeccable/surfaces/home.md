# Surface brief — Home and discovery

Mode: Operate. Scope: GameVault home, catalog discovery, game records, and personal library.

Audience and task: gamers browse provider-backed modern and classic games, open a record, and track progress without losing their own choices. Success is a truthful, usable path from catalog to personal log. Content is external RAWG data only when configured; personal state belongs to its authenticated owner. PS2 and PS3 must remain explicit destinations. No deployment target or source brand assets were supplied; the user-provided visual reference guides the direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the home feel like a cinematic game shelf you can explore, while preserving the catalog's truthful data and the collector's direct paths into each game.

OWN-WORLD: Keep GameVault's graphite field, high-contrast light type, vivid lime actions, and real RAWG artwork. Cinematic imagery and landscape rails change the composition while preserving the catalog's direct platform paths.

STORY: Visitors land on featured games with provider artwork, catalog facts, and existing actions, then browse platform shortcuts and horizontal catalog rails. PS2 and PS3 remain explicit destinations. Signed-in users see only their own playing, backlog, and favorites shelves.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wide, image-led feature fills the stage, with a deep graphite treatment behind the title and actions; a landscape rail begins the browse journey below it. Keep the console shortcuts and every existing rail reachable, with no fabricated catalog content.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Each rail supports trackpad/touch scrolling, visible previous/next controls, and left/right keyboard scrolling. Focus stays on real links and controls; focused cards remain visible and legible at every breakpoint.

STATES: Keep provider failures distinct from empty shelves, retain the page-level loading skeleton, and show honest fallback copy when RAWG artwork or catalog content is unavailable.

FORM: Streaming-home composition adapted to the established collector's catalog. Code-led; use RAWG imagery and existing @phosphor-icons/react/ssr controls, with no generated or sample game art.

FINISH: Follow the supplied graphite-and-lime visual reference, keeping the catalog facts and owner-private records truthful. Verify the home at desktop and mobile sizes, preserve horizontal rails and existing routes, and do not claim a visual review without current screenshots.
